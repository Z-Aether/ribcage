Welcome

Ribcage is where the group plans and runs one film marathon: suggest films, vote on them, and follow the evening.

The five phases

The marathon moves through five phases, and you act mainly in SUGGEST and VOTE.

  1. SUGGEST: everyone puts forward films, each with a line on why.
  2. APPROVE: the admin chooses which suggestions go to the vote. You can still read the pool and add notes.
  3. VOTE: you score the films on the ballot and veto any you won't be in the room for.
  4. PICK: voting closes and everyone's scores open, by name, while the admin chooses the evening's films.
  5. RUN: the evening itself, with what's on now and what's next.

Content warnings

You can name topics you want to be warned about, and films that carry them are marked for you alone. That list is private to you in the app.

The admin and the host of the server can technically read it, as they can read everything stored here. It is deleted when the marathon is torn down.
